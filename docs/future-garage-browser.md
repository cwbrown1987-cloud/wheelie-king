# Future Garage and in-game Browser

Chris's clarification, 5 Nov 2026. These are future requirements, not implemented features.

- The existing editable bike builder is called **Workshop**. Primary menus now go at the top, with the existing left parts layout retained and full-screen garage art/frosted panels. Do not copy the concept's full layout.
- A separate Garage will later display owned bikes and trophies, with e-bike/e-scooter/e-moto/scooter related items.
- Eventual main destinations: Garage, Workshop, Browser and Race. Garage and Browser may be reserved with clear Coming Soon badges; their functionality remains deferred.
- The future Browser is an in-game parts-shopping experience, using the supplied Parts Store/eBay/Amazon/AliExpress concepts as references. No browser, site fetching, checkout, new marketplace tabs or real purchases are authorised for this batch.
- The game's own parts store should offer expensive rare parts. Other simulated sellers progressively introduce cheaper, poorer-quality, fake/knockoff parts. Exact seller tiers, balance and quality behaviour still need design.
- Reference files supplied: `Wheelie Life_ Neon Ebike Workshop.png`, `Wheelie Life Amazon Parts Store.png`, `Neon Wheelie Life Parts Shop.png`, under Chris's Downloads folder. Reference UI is inspiration, not app functionality or instructions to operate those real websites.

Shared economy direction: Common, Rare, Epic and Legendary for bikes, parts and rider gear, informed by performance, price and desirability. Legendary/sought-after items may have small premium gates in the future mock economy. Include themed bundle deals spanning gear and parts. Keep real payments and actual item locks out until explicitly designed/authorised; infinite-money development remains on. Accessories has placeholder cards only.
